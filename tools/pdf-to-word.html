PDF to Word Converter — Free, Editable .docx

Pull the text out of a PDF into an editable Word document. Your file never leaves your device — everything happens in your browser, nothing is uploaded to any server. It keeps headings, bold text, headers, footers and images where the PDF has them, and rebuilds tables as real Word tables when the columns line up cleanly — the exact layout and spacing are not preserved.

Learn: how to use this well

  1. Best for typed PDFs. Reports, articles, e-books and letters created on a computer convert cleanly. Scanned PDFs are pictures of text — they need OCR, which this tool does not do (it tells you when a PDF has no readable text).
  2. Expect to tidy up. Headings, bold text and images carry over, but columns and table borders come out as plain paragraphs in reading order. Budget a few minutes to polish the layout in Word.
  3. Two-column layouts read across. Academic papers with two columns may interleave lines. Short of OCR software, selecting and copying from the original PDF column by column is the workaround.
  4. Check numbers carefully. Amounts, dates and ID numbers are the details that matter most in converted documents — verify them against the original.

Pro tips

  • Converting your own old report back to Word beats retyping it — even an imperfect conversion is 90% of the work done.
  • For a CV locked in PDF, convert, then rebuild it properly with the Resume Builder instead of fighting the layout.
  • Keep the original PDF beside the Word file until you have checked the full text once.

Frequently asked questions

Is my PDF uploaded to a server?

No. The text is extracted inside your browser. Your file never leaves your device.

Why does the Word file look different from the PDF?

The converter rebuilds the document from the PDF's text and pictures: headings, bold text, alignment, headers, footers and images are carried over, but the exact visual layout — fonts, columns and table borders — is not. For a faithful visual copy, the PDF itself is the document to share.

Why did it find no text?

Your PDF is most likely scanned images. Scanned pages are photographs of text and need OCR (optical character recognition), which this free tool honestly does not offer.

Can it convert a password-protected PDF?

No. Remove the password first if the document is yours, then convert.
