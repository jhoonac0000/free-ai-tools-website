Image Compressor — to Exact KB Size

Shrink a photo to the exact size a form demands — 25 KB for application portals, 50 KB, 100 KB, or any custom target. Your file never leaves your device — everything happens in your browser, nothing is uploaded to any server.

Learn: how to use this well

  1. Check the portal's exact limit first. Many application and exam portals reject photos above 25 KB or 30 KB, and some also demand exact pixel dimensions. Hit the size here, then use the Image Resizer if the pixels must match too.
  2. Start from the best original you have. Compression removes detail. A sharp, well-lit original compressed to 25 KB looks better than a blurry photo at any size.
  3. Plain backgrounds compress smaller. Busy backgrounds (trees, crowds, patterns) hold detail the algorithm must keep. A plain wall behind the subject reaches tiny sizes without hurting the face.
  4. JPEG for photos, WebP for the web. JPEG is accepted everywhere, including government portals. WebP is smaller still but some older systems refuse it — when in doubt, JPEG.
  5. Never compress the only copy. Keep your original untouched and upload the compressed copy. You cannot get the removed detail back.

Pro tips

  • If a portal asks for 25 KB and a passport-style photo, crop tight to the head and shoulders first — less background means more quality left for the face.
  • Scanned documents compress worse than camera photos. If a scan will not shrink, re-scan at 150–200 DPI instead of 600.
  • Test the upload on the portal before the deadline day. Portals measure size strictly, and a 25.4 KB file fails a 25 KB limit.

Frequently asked questions

Is my photo uploaded to your server?

No. The compression runs entirely in your browser using your own device. Your photo never leaves your computer or phone, and nothing is stored anywhere.

Why did the dimensions change?

To reach very small targets like 25 KB, quality reduction alone is sometimes not enough, so the tool also shrinks the pixel dimensions step by step until the file fits. It always reports the final size before you download.

Can I compress to exactly 25 KB?

The tool targets at or just under your chosen size — the closest it can get while keeping the most quality. File formats cannot promise an exact byte count, but the result will be within your limit.

Does it work for PNG images?

Yes — PNG photos are converted to JPEG or WebP, because PNG cannot reach small sizes for photographs. Logos with transparency should use the Image Converter instead if transparency must be kept.
