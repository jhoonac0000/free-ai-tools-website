Word to PDF — .docx Converter

Turn a Word document into a share-ready PDF. Your file never leaves your device — everything happens in your browser, nothing is uploaded to any server. Formatting stays close to the original for normal documents; complex tables, text boxes and exact page breaks can differ.

Learn: how to use this well

  1. PDF is how documents travel. A PDF looks the same on every phone and computer and cannot be edited by accident. Always send the PDF; keep the .docx as your editable master.
  2. Only .docx works. Old .doc files (Word 97–2003) must first be saved as .docx — in Word: File → Save As → Word Document (.docx). Google Docs does it too: File → Download is the wrong direction; open the .doc and re-save it.
  3. Check the PDF page by page. Fonts your document uses may be substituted, which can move line breaks. Headings, page numbers and tables deserve one careful look before you send.
  4. Images travel, exotic features may not. Photos and basic tables convert well. Text boxes, SmartArt and tracked-changes displays can simplify or drop — remove them or accept a Word-exported PDF for those files.

Pro tips

  • Submitting a CV? Convert to PDF and name it “Firstname-Lastname-CV.pdf” — recruiters' systems handle PDFs best.
  • If a long document's pages split mid-paragraph, add page breaks in Word before converting; they are respected.
  • Preview on your phone too — that is the screen most readers will actually use.

Frequently asked questions

Is my document uploaded anywhere?

No. The conversion runs in your browser. Your document never leaves your device.

Why .docx only, not .doc?

.doc is an old binary format from the 1990s that browser libraries cannot read reliably. Saving as .docx takes ten seconds in any modern word processor.

Will my formatting be identical?

For normal documents — headings, paragraphs, lists, basic tables, images — it stays close. It is not a pixel-perfect copy: unusual fonts, text boxes and complex layouts can shift. Always give the PDF one check before sending something important.

Can I convert back to Word afterwards?

The PDF is for sharing, not editing. Keep your .docx — and if you ever lose it, our PDF to Word tool can recover the text.

Earn: how this skill pays

“Please send it as a PDF” is in every job post, tender and admission notice. Professionals convert as a reflex; beginners lose marks and interviews over an un-openable .docx. If you prepare documents or applications for others, a clean, correctly named PDF is part of the product you are selling.
