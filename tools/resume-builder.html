    Learn: how to use this well

    1. Pick the template for the job. Modern and Executive suit creative and customer-facing roles; Classic ATS is the safest for big companies that scan resumes with software; Minimal suits academic and technical roles.
    2. Watch the live preview. Everything updates as you type — if your resume spills past one page, cut the oldest or least relevant lines first.
    3. Use numbers. "Handled 40 customers a day" beats "responsible for customers". Numbers make claims believable, and the strength score checks for them.
    4. Match the job ad's words. If the ad says "customer support", use those exact words in your skills — many companies filter by keywords first.
    5. One page if you can. Hiring managers scan resumes in seconds. Two pages only when the extra page adds real proof, not filler.
    6. Proofread twice. One typo can end an application. Read it out loud once before you download the PDF.

    Pro tips

    • Save your resume as a PDF with a clear file name: YourName-Role.pdf.
    • Your work saves automatically in this browser — come back later on the same device and it is still here.
    • Keep a master resume with everything, then delete lines to make each one-page tailored version.

    Frequently asked questions

    Is this resume builder really free?

    Yes — every template, the live preview, the strength score and the PDF download are free. There is no account, no payment, no watermark and no locked download: the tricks some big builders use (free to build, paid to download) are not used here.

    Where does my information go?

    Nowhere. Everything you type — including your photo — stays on your device and is saved only in your own browser's storage. We have no server receiving your resume — see our Privacy page.

    Which template is best for ATS screening software?

    Classic ATS: a single column, standard headings and no photo, which is the easiest layout for screening software to read. Whichever template you pick, always send the PDF version, not a photo of your resume.

    Should my resume be one page or two?

    One page for most jobs under 10 years of experience. The live preview is a true A4 page, so you can see exactly when your content passes one page.

    Can I use this for a specific niche, like nursing?

    Yes. Put the niche in your target role and add one line of niche proof (licence, ward experience, patient numbers) in the summary and certificates sections.
