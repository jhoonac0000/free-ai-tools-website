Blog Title + Outline Generator

One topic in, eight titles and a full outline out. Built from proven article frameworks in your browser — no AI server is involved.

Learn: how to use this well

  1. Outline before you write. An outline turns a blank page into eight small jobs. Most writer's block is just a missing outline.
  2. One idea per section. If a section needs two ideas, it is two sections. Readers follow one thread at a time.
  3. Put the reader's problem in the intro. The first paragraph should say: this is your problem, this article solves it, here is how.
  4. End every section with something usable. A tip, a check, a question — never just information that stops.
  5. FAQ sections answer real searches. The questions readers actually type are the easiest traffic an article can earn.
  6. Conclude with actions, not summary. "Your first 3 actions this week" beats "In conclusion, we discussed..." every time.

Pro tips

  • Write the outline, then leave it for a day. Weak sections reveal themselves on the second look.
  • Generate titles first, outline second — the title you pick changes what the article must deliver.
  • For SEO, use the topic's plain words in the title and the first heading. Clever wordplay hides articles from search.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use the outline as-is for a real article?

Yes — that is what it is for. Fill each section in your own words, add your own examples, and it becomes an original article.

How long should the finished article be?

Long enough to answer the title's promise, no longer. Most useful guides land between 800 and 2,000 words.

Will two people get the same outline?

The framework is shared, but your topic, audience and examples make the finished article yours. Always write the sections yourself.

Is this an AI writer?

No. It builds titles and outlines from proven frameworks in your browser. The writing itself is yours — which is exactly why it stays original.
