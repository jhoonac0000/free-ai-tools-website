Instagram Caption + Bio Generator

One topic, one tone — five captions, a bio and hashtags. Framework-based, in your browser; no AI server is involved.

Learn: how to use this well

  1. First line first. Instagram cuts captions after two lines. Put the hook — not the hashtag — at the top.
  2. One idea per caption. A caption that tries to say five things says nothing. Pick the feeling of the photo and write to it.
  3. End with a question or a nudge. "What is your version of this?" gets more comments than a statement does.
  4. Bio: what, for whom, where next. Three short pieces, one link. If a stranger cannot tell what you do in three seconds, rewrite it.
  5. Hashtags: relevant, not many. Ten real tags about the topic beat thirty popular tags that have nothing to do with the post.
  6. Keep your tone consistent. Followers follow a voice, not just photos. Pick a tone and stay in it for a month before judging it.

Pro tips

  • Write captions the night before, not while posting — rushed captions read rushed.
  • Save your best captions in a note; reuse the structure, change the details.
  • For business accounts, every fifth post should help or teach — not just sell.

Frequently asked questions

How long should a caption be?

As long as the idea needs — but the first two lines must work alone, because that is all most people see before tapping "more".

Do hashtags still work?

Yes, but relevance matters more than quantity. Tags should describe the actual post, not chase the biggest tags.

Can I use these for Reels too?

Yes. Short captions work best for Reels — pick the shortest option and let the video carry the rest.

Will my topic be stored anywhere?

No. Everything runs in your browser and is forgotten when you close the page.
