Image Converter — JPG · PNG · WebP

Convert any picture between JPG, PNG and WebP. Your file never leaves your device — everything happens in your browser, nothing is uploaded to any server.

Learn: how to use this well

  1. JPG is for photos. Small files, accepted everywhere, no transparency. The default choice for anything a portal, marketplace or email asks for.
  2. PNG is for graphics and transparency. Logos, screenshots with text and anything with a transparent background stay crisp in PNG — at a bigger file size.
  3. WebP is for your own website. Same quality as JPG at a noticeably smaller size, which makes pages load faster. All modern browsers support it.
  4. Converting to JPG removes transparency. Transparent areas are placed on white. If a logo must stay transparent, convert to PNG or WebP instead.
  5. Converting up does not add quality. A blurry JPG converted to PNG is still blurry — just bigger. Convert from the best original you have.

Pro tips

  • Screenshots for documents: PNG keeps text readable where JPG gets fuzzy around letters.
  • Product photos for a shop: WebP at quality 0.85 is the sweet spot most stores use.
  • Keep one master copy in PNG; make JPG/WebP copies for each place you publish.

Frequently asked questions

Is my image uploaded anywhere?

No. Conversion happens in your browser with your own device. Nothing is uploaded or stored.

Why is my PNG bigger than the JPG?

PNG keeps every pixel exactly, which is heavy for photographs. That is expected — PNG is for graphics, screenshots and transparency, JPG/WebP are for photos.

Can I convert a WebP back to JPG?

Yes — JPG, PNG and WebP can all be converted to any of the other two.

What happens to transparent backgrounds?

Converting to JPG fills transparency with white, because JPG cannot store transparency. PNG and WebP keep it.
