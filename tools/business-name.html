Business Name + Slogan Generator

One keyword in, eight name ideas and five slogans out. Built from naming frameworks in your browser — no AI server is involved.

Learn: how to use this well

  1. Short beats clever. Two words people can spell after hearing once beat a clever name nobody can spell.
  2. Say it out loud. If you stumble saying it, customers will too. Names live in conversation, not just on logos.
  3. Check the obvious places. Before you fall in love with a name, check the domain, the social handles and a trademark search in your country.
  4. Meaning over decoration. A name that hints at what you do ("Bright Coffee") works harder than a pretty word that means nothing.
  5. Slogan = promise, not poetry. The best slogans say what changes for the customer: "Coffee, done right" beats "Taste the dream".
  6. Sleep on the shortlist. A name you still like after three days is a better bet than one you loved for three minutes.

Pro tips

  • Ask five strangers what business they think the name is. If most guess wrong, keep looking.
  • Avoid names that lock you into one product — you may expand later.
  • Pick the name with a free domain and matching social handle, even if it is your second favourite.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use these names for a real business?

They are starting ideas, not legal clearance. Always check domain availability, social handles and trademarks before registering anything.

Why do some names sound similar?

Because good naming follows patterns — a strong word plus your keyword, or your keyword plus a trade word. The patterns are the point; your keyword makes each result yours.

Should the name say what I sell?

Usually yes, at least a hint. Purely abstract names work for big brands with big budgets; small businesses benefit from clarity.

What makes a slogan good?

One promise, in plain words, that a customer could repeat. If it needs explaining, it is not a slogan yet.
