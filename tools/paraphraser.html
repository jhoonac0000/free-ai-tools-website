Paraphraser + Grammar Checker

Paste a sentence or a paragraph. The paraphraser swaps common words for synonyms, and the checker flags the mistakes people actually make. Word-bank based, in your browser — it does not use an AI server.

Learn: how to use this well

  1. Paraphrase the idea, not just the words. Read the sentence, look away, say it your way, then compare. Swapping one word at a time keeps the original sentence's shape — and its problems.
  2. Keep meaning first. A synonym that changes the meaning is worse than the original word. If you are unsure, keep the original.
  3. Fix grammar before style. A clean, plain sentence beats a fancy sentence with a mistake in it.
  4. Read it out loud. If you stumble, the reader will too. Shorten that sentence.
  5. Never paraphrase to hide copying. Reworded text still needs credit when the idea is not yours. Paraphrasing is a writing tool, not a way around plagiarism.

Pro tips

  • For ESL writing, paraphrase one paragraph at a time and check each against the original meaning.
  • Strong verbs beat fancy adjectives: "build" usually beats "very good creation".
  • Run the checker, fix everything it flags, then read once more yourself — no checker catches everything.

Frequently asked questions

Is this as good as a big paraphrasing app?

Honestly, no — large apps use language models we do not run here. This tool is free, private and good for everyday rewording; for publish-ready work, edit the result yourself.

Does paraphrasing avoid plagiarism?

No. Changing words without crediting the source is still plagiarism. Paraphrase to express ideas in your own voice, and cite the source when the idea is not yours.

What grammar mistakes does it catch?

Common ones: double spaces, repeated words, classic typos ("teh", "recieve"), missing full stops, and lowercase "i". It is a first pass, not a full proofreader.

Is my text private?

Yes. Everything runs in your browser; nothing is sent to any server.
