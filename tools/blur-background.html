Blur Tool — Image & Background Blur

Blur a whole photo, or keep the person sharp and blur only what is behind them. Your file never leaves your device — everything happens in your browser, nothing is uploaded to any server. Background mode detects the person with an on-device model (one-time download).

Learn: how to use this well

  1. Blur is privacy, not just style. Faces of bystanders, house numbers, documents on a desk, a screen in the background — a ten-second blur protects people who never agreed to be in your photo.
  2. Background blur focuses the story. A sharp subject on a soft background is the portrait look phones charge extra for. It works best when the subject is clearly separated from what is behind them.
  3. Less is usually more. 8–15 px hides clutter and protects privacy while the photo still looks natural. Maximum blur looks like a censor bar.
  4. Check the edges at full size. Detection can miss hair strands or blur a shoulder. If one photo matters, look at it closely before publishing.

Pro tips

  • Blurring a screenshot's chat names before sharing it publicly is a habit worth having.
  • For product listings, a light background blur makes plain rooms look intentional instead of messy.
  • Never rely on blur to hide something that must truly stay secret in a legal document — crop it out instead; blur can sometimes be partially reversed.

Frequently asked questions

Is my photo uploaded anywhere?

No. Both blur modes run in your browser. The people-detection model used by Background mode downloads to your device once; your photo itself never leaves it.

Background mode did not start. Why?

The detection model needs a decent connection for its one-time download and a modern browser. If it cannot load, the tool tells you and Whole-image mode still works.

Why did part of the person get blurred?

Detection is automatic and not perfect — loose hair, hats and arms held away from the body can be misread. A clearer photo with the subject well separated from the background detects better.

What format is the download?

PNG, at the original dimensions, so no quality is lost beyond the blur itself.
