Frequently asked questions

Quick answers before you start.

Are ToolNest tools really free?

Yes. All 15 tools are free to use, as often as you like. Premium template packs are planned for the store later, but the free tools stay free — a premium pack only adds more designs, it never locks a basic tool away.

Do I need to create an account?

No. There is no sign-up and no email wall. Open any tool and use it straight away.

Is my text or data uploaded to a server?

No. Every tool runs entirely in your browser, on your own device. What you type into a tool is not sent to us or stored anywhere. See the Privacy Policy for details.

How do I save my result?

Most tools give you a Copy button. For documents like resumes and invoices, use the Print button and choose “Save as PDF” in your browser's print dialog.

Can I use the results for paid client work?

Yes. The resumes, invoices, descriptions and other results you create with ToolNest are yours to use, including for paying clients.

Do the calculators give financial advice?

No. The loan, mortgage, tax and rate calculators give educational estimates only, so you can plan and compare. For decisions that matter, check with a qualified professional.

Premium packs — coming to the store

The free tools above stay free. Premium template packs (resume designs, invoice templates, description bundles) are planned for the store — the free tool always comes first, and the premium version only adds more designs, never locks the basic tool away.
